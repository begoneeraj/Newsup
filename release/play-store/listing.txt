Full description:
Explore Indian news with context, evidence and source links.

TruthLens India brings together news claims, AI-assisted analysis and coverage of longer-running public issues. Review verdict labels, read supporting sources, follow developing crises and save items on your device.

Features include:
• AI-assisted claim analysis with confidence indicators and source links.
• Crisis tracking and coverage of public-interest issues.
• Government-promise tracking and science/data sections.
• Local bookmarks and appearance controls.
• Optional notifications, enabled only when you choose.
• Accessible privacy information, corrections contact and account-deletion controls.

AI-generated summaries, labels and scores can be inaccurate or incomplete. They are not guarantees of truth or human expert review. Read the original linked sources before relying on a claim. Unverified claims are not established facts.

TruthLens India is an independent service published by Pushpraj singh prashant. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India. Reporting belongs to its original publishers.
